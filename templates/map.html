Tips & Tricks

Route Planning Tips

  • Click directly on the map to set start/end points instead of typing addresses
  • Use the Swap button to quickly reverse your route direction
  • Compare multiple routes to find the best balance of time vs distance
  • Check traffic predictions before important trips to avoid delays

Time Optimization

  • Leave 15-30 minutes earlier than rush hour (7:30 AM instead of 8:00 AM)
  • Weekend traffic patterns are typically lighter and more predictable
  • Use Smart Planner for airport runs and important meetings
  • Avoid 5:00-6:30 PM for the heaviest evening congestion

Analytics Insights

  • Congestion over 70% indicates heavy traffic - consider alternative routes
  • Average speed below 20 km/h suggests city center traffic
  • Monitor patterns to understand your regular route's traffic flow
  • Export data to analyze commute patterns over time

Keyboard Shortcuts

  • ESC - Close any modal window
  • Ctrl+C - Clear current route
  • Ctrl+S - Save current route
  • Space - Toggle traffic predictions

Mobile Tips

  • Pinch to zoom for better map navigation
  • Long press on map to set route points
  • Swipe panels to access different controls
  • Landscape mode provides better route visibility

Getting Started

How do I plan my first route?

1. Click on the map or enter a start location
2. Enter your destination
3. Click "Find Route" to see optimized options
4. Choose the best route for your needs

What do the colors on the map mean?

Green = Light traffic (fast)
Yellow/Orange = Moderate traffic
Red = Heavy traffic (slow)
